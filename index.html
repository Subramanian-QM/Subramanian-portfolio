I started as a Quality Engineer with a degree in Mechanical Engineering, curious about one thing: why do customers reject what we work so hard to build? That question never left me.

Over two decades, across manufacturing, logistics, corporate groups, projects and services in India, Qatar and Saudi Arabia, I have seen quality at its best and at its most ignored. I have designed and delivered 250+ quality courses, from induction for new joiners to awareness programmes for senior leadership.

Too many organisations treat quality as a compliance checkbox. I have spent two decades pushing back on that thinking, making sure excellence is built in, not bolted on.

Today that work is on a major airport infrastructure programme in Riyadh, where I govern quality on behalf of the client: auditing, closing out non-conformances and reporting performance to senior management.
